# Emille

Emille is a meal planning app for keeping recipes, deciding what to cook, and turning a meal plan into a shopping list.

**Use the app:** [emille.app](https://emille.app)

## What you can do

- Save recipes or import them from a URL.
- Plan meals for the week.
- Add ingredients from meals to a shopping list.
- Follow recipes step by step while cooking.
- Share meals and coordinate with friends.

Emille also has optional AI features for reviewing recipe steps and organizing shopping lists by store section.
